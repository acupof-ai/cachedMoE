Reading it
----------
* the union sizes are MEASURED and they reproduce tools/route_union.py's offline
  u(M) to within 2%: 9.90 / 13.35 / 16.53 / 19.35 / 22.13 against 9.88 / 13.22 /
  16.23 / 19.02 / 21.61.
* forward_batch at M = 1 is 1.21x SLOWER than a decode step (123.7 vs 102.6):
  one more submit+fence a layer, no MOE_OVERLAP, the union's host staging, and
  the engram run row by row.
* at M = 6 a verify batch costs 0.74x six sequential positions (75.9 vs 102.6).
  docs/p4_dspark_runtime.md 6.5 projected 0.62x from the kernel tables; the gap
  is the host overhead above.
* P0 goes to 0: at a 128-token context with 5,100 slots the whole working set is
  resident, so this table is the COMPUTE-BOUND regime. A real conversation is
  NVMe-bound (STATUS 4: 273 of 274 ms is the drive), so this answers "what is
  the kernel-side 0.62x worth IF decode stops being I/O bound", which is the
  condition docs/p4_dspark_runtime.md 6.5 item 4 left open.
